Hero video (optional)

Add these two files and redeploy. The video fades in over the drawn balustrade backdrop:

  hero.mp4          - one 16:9 MP4 (H.264, no audio, 1280x720, about 2-3 MB). Keep the subject in the middle third of the frame so it also crops well on phones.
  hero-poster.jpg   - the first frame, shown while the video loads.

If hero.mp4 is not here, the site simply shows the drawn backdrop. Nothing breaks.
